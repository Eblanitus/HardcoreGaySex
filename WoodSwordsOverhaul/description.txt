Part of the HardcoreMelee series. Wooden swords become sticks, plus sharpened daggers.

Every stick and dagger knows four moves:
- LMB: swing. A light arc with tiny damage that shoves enemies back. Control, not DPS.
- Tap RMB: brace as a spear. Damage grows with closing speed: run into the enemy, or let it run into you.
- Hold RMB: quick stabs.
- RMB + LMB: throw. No auto-return: the stick stays in soft ground or in the enemy until you walk up and pick it up.

Daggers are made from sticks (one stick, one dagger, at a Work Bench) and can't be turned back. Sticks are stronger at swinging and bracing, daggers at stabbing.

Compatible with Weapon Out Lite.
